Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Impawise Inc. ("Impawise", "we", "us") makes games for mobile devices. This policy explains what information our apps and this website handle. The short version: we don't collect personal information.

Information our apps collect

Our games, including Slow Blink, do not require an account. They do not collect, store or share personal information, and they contain no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Game progress and settings are saved only on your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells us only that a purchase succeeded, so we can unlock what you bought. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you. We don't sell or share it, and we delete support correspondence when it's no longer needed.

Children

Our games are suitable for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@impawise.com.
