Silk Cam

A camera for natural, no-makeup selfies.

Silk Cam is an iPhone camera that makes you look rested, not retouched. It gently softens skin while keeping its real texture, adds a little brightness, lifts shadows under the eyes and very slightly firms the jawline. It's the look of light makeup that looks like no makeup.

You see the effect live as you frame the shot. Turn it off at any time to compare, pick a colour style such as Warm, Soft or Film, and every photo saves straight to your Photos library.

Our approach to Silk Cam

Natural first

Pores, freckles and the shape of your face stay yours. The effect is tuned to be noticed only when you compare.

Simple

One carefully tuned look and a handful of colour styles. No sliders to fiddle with.

Private by design

Everything happens on your iPhone. Your photos and face are never uploaded, and the app has no ads, accounts or tracking.

Help and privacy

Questions about the app? Visit our Support page. Silk Cam collects no personal information. See the Silk Cam Privacy Policy.
