Slow Blink

A game about introducing two cats who don't get along yet.

Bringing a new cat home is exciting until the resident cat hisses at the door. In Slow Blink, you read their moods, give them space, swap scents and rooms, and let trust build one moment at a time.

The lesson is a true one: rushing an introduction backfires, and patience works. We built the game on the methods real cat owners use, and on our own two cats, who went through exactly this.

Our approach to Slow Blink

Patience over pressure

The game rewards slowing down and paying attention. There are no timers nagging you and no streaks to protect.

Learn by playing

Every mechanic is grounded in real cat behavior, so what you learn in the game helps at home.

Respect for players

No ads, no tracking, and no manipulative design. Optional purchases are clearly labeled and never required to finish.

Help and privacy

Questions about the game? Visit our Support page. Slow Blink collects no personal information. See our Privacy Policy.
