Slow Blink

Bringing a new cat home is exciting until the resident cat hisses at the door. Slow Blink is a cozy strategy game about introducing two cats who don't get along yet. Read their moods, give them space, swap scents and rooms, and let trust build one calm moment at a time.

The lesson is a true one: rushing an introduction backfires, and patience works. We built the game on the methods real cat owners use, and on our own two cats, who went through exactly this.

  • Platform: iPhone
  • Genre: Cozy strategy & learning
  • For: Cat lovers and would-be second-cat owners
  • Status: In development

Our approach

Patience over pressure

Our games reward slowing down and paying attention. There are no timers nagging you and no streaks to protect.

Learn by playing

Every mechanic is grounded in real animal behavior, so what you learn in the game helps at home.

Respect for players

No ads, no tracking, and no manipulative design. Optional purchases are clearly labeled and never required to finish.

What's next

Slow Blink is our first title. We're already sketching more games about animals and the people who love them, and in time we plan to offer products that help pet owners in everyday life.
