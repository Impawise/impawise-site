Frequently asked questions

When will Slow Blink be released?

Slow Blink is in development. We'll announce the release date here when it's ready.

Does Slow Blink need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game doesn't require an account, and your progress is saved on your device.

I bought something in the game but didn't receive it.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. First, fully close and reopen the game. If the item still isn't there, email us with the approximate date of purchase. You can also request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all App Store refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

Will my progress transfer to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on your device, so it moves with an iPhone backup or device-to-device transfer.

Where does Silk Cam save my photos?

Straight to your Photos library. Silk Cam doesn't keep its own copies, and nothing is uploaded.

Silk Cam shows a black screen or can't save photos.

Silk Cam needs permission to use the camera and to add photos. Open Settings, choose Silk Cam, and turn on Camera and Photos.

Can I turn the beauty effect off?

Yes. Tap the button in the top corner to switch between the natural look and your unedited camera. Colour styles work either way.
