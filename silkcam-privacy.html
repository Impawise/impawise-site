Silk Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers Silk Cam, an iPhone camera app by Impawise Inc. ("Impawise", "we", "us"). The short version: Silk Cam does not collect, store or share any of your data. Our general Privacy Policy covers this website and contacting us.

Camera and photos

Silk Cam uses your camera to show the live preview and to take photos. Each photo is saved only to your own Photos library, and only after you give permission. Photos are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Face information

To place its effects, Silk Cam finds the position of facial features, such as the outline of the face, eyes and lips, using Apple's on-device Vision framework. This happens entirely on your iPhone, one camera frame at a time, and the information is discarded immediately. It is never stored, never leaves your device, and is never used to identify you, for advertising or for any other purpose.

What Silk Cam doesn't do

Silk Cam has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. It does not connect to the internet. The only thing it remembers is your chosen colour style, which is stored on your device.

Children

Silk Cam doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through our Support page.
