About Impawise

A small studio with a soft spot for animals.

Our story

Impawise began at home. When we brought a second cat into a household with a very territorial first cat, the hissing, hiding and standoffs taught us more about patience than we expected. Slowly, with scent swaps, separate rooms and a lot of quiet waiting, the two cats learned to share a home.

Many people go through the same thing, and many give up too soon because no one told them that slow is the fast way. We started Impawise to turn lessons like that into games that are calm, honest and fun.

What we believe

  • Games can teach without lecturing. The best lessons come from playing.
  • Animals deserve accuracy. We research real behavior before we design around it.
  • Players deserve respect. We don't run ads, collect personal data or use dark patterns.
